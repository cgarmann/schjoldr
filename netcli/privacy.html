Privacy Policy

Data Controller

SCHJOLDR is the data controller for personal data collected through the NetCLI app. Contact: support@schjoldr.io

What we collect

  • Email address (when creating an account, or provided by Google / Apple when you sign in with them)
  • Display name (optional, set by the user or taken from your Google / Apple account)
  • Selected profile avatar (stored locally and in the cloud)
  • Learning data: quiz results, exam results, streaks and progress
  • Terminal Lab command history — the last 50 commands you type into the simulated Cisco CLI, stored so the session survives a restart. This is free text; please do not type real passwords or personal information into the practice terminal.
  • Saved Custom Lab topologies, including any names you give devices
  • Preferences: theme and language settings

The Android and iOS apps have optional usage analytics. They send usage events to Google Firebase only after you choose Allow usage analytics. Events include screen names, built-in question/exercise identifiers, results, hint use, time spent and steps in the Enable purchase flow, such as seeing a price, tapping Buy and the purchase outcome. Firebase also processes app-instance and device/app information. We do not attach your NetCLI account ID, name, email, payment details, terminal text or lab contents. Advertising ID collection and advertising consent are disabled. With the same choice on, if Android stopped the app because it froze, the next start sends one error report to Google Firebase Firestore: the frozen thread's code stack, app version, Android version and phone model, without your account or anything you typed. Turn collection off in Profile; this stops new collection and resets local Analytics data, but does not delete events already sent.

Purpose and legal basis

Account and saved-learning data is used to provide the service. Optional usage analytics (Android and iOS) is separate, runs only after your opt-in, and is shared with Google Firebase to help improve learning activities and the Enable purchase flow. You can withdraw that choice in Profile. We do not sell your personal data.

Data processors

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and, when you opt in, Analytics) for user authentication and data storage. Google acts as a data processor under a data processing agreement with SCHJOLDR. Data may be stored on servers in the EU/EEA or the US under EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC). See Google's privacy policy.

Purchases of NetCLI Enable are processed by RevenueCat, Inc., which verifies store receipts and provides purchase history. RevenueCat receives your opaque NetCLI account identifier and purchase/device information to link purchases across your devices; we do not send your name or email for this purpose. If you are not signed in, the Enable page and purchase screen ask the store for the price using a random anonymous identifier that is not linked to you. NetCLI calculates and verifies your access period on the server. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple or Google; SCHJOLDR never sees your card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you sign in with Apple, Apple provides your name and email address (or a private relay address if you choose to hide it) to create the account. See Apple's privacy policy.

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. This means your IP address and browser user-agent are sent to Google each time the fonts are fetched.

On Android, the app may request age-related signals from Google Play where required by law. This may include an age range for the account holder. The signals are stored only on your device, are never transmitted to SCHJOLDR or any third party, and are used solely to provide age-appropriate access — not for marketing, profiling or analytics.

Your device uses localStorage to store settings and learning data locally. Essential local learning storage is separate from the optional Analytics choice.

NetCLI Enable drill sessions and recent answer history are stored only on this device, separately for the guest and each account. They are not uploaded through learning-progress sync. If you opt in to usage analytics, selected question identifiers, outcomes and timing are reported as usage events; the full local question/answer history is not uploaded. Signing out or deleting an account does not clear this local practice history; clear the app's device storage when you no longer need it.

Retention

Optional Analytics events are not linked to your NetCLI account. Their retention follows the Google Analytics project settings. Turning analytics off or deleting your NetCLI account does not automatically delete events already sent to Google.

Data is stored as long as the account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app under Profile → Delete account. Deletion removes the signed-in account and its stored account data after identity verification. Device labs and other accounts on the device are kept. The deletion screen offers a device recovery copy of the open lab, notes and terminal history; other account labs should be saved or exported before deletion.

Deleting your account permanently forfeits remaining NetCLI Enable access and any open upgrade. Purchases cannot be restored to a new NetCLI account. Account-linked purchase records and the RevenueCat customer are removed automatically, with automatic retries during service outages. NetCLI retains only anonymised, keyed transaction fingerprints to prevent the same purchase being credited again; they contain no account identifier or receipt. Apple and Google retain their own purchase records under their retention terms. Account deletion does not refund purchases.

The server checks the upgrade before payment and again when the payment arrives. An upgrade that cannot be applied (for example because the month ended before the payment completed) gives no extra access and is refunded: Google Play purchases automatically, App Store purchases through Apple's refund request, which the app opens for you. If the one-month purchase is refunded, its upgrade ends too and is refunded the same way. Review the localised store price before confirming.
